The project

Wake up facing the Mediterranean

Amanecer is a residential complex of three buildings on Torre la Sal beach — one of the best family beaches on the coast, surrounded by protected natural areas. Next door is Magic World / Marina d'Or with water parks, restaurants and Europe's largest sea-water spa.

  • Pool with separate adults' and children's areas, LED lighting and 3 hydromassage spas
  • Social lounge for residents and landscaped pedestrian area
  • Lobby with 3 lifts, underground and ground-floor parking, EV charging pre-installation
  • Air conditioning (hot/cold), aerothermal hot water, motorised blinds, double glazing
  • Apartments are handed over with furniture, appliances, TVs and kitchenware — ready to move in or rent out
